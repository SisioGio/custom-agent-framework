# Accounting Manual – Accounts Payable (AP)

## 1. Objective
Define and document the standard procedures for processing supplier invoices, payments, and expense reimbursements to ensure compliance, accuracy, and timely disbursements.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all company locations and departments submitting supplier invoices or payment requests.

## 3. Key Responsibilities
- **AP Clerk:** Invoice entry and matching
- **AP Supervisor:** Review, approval, and reconciliation
- **Finance Manager:** Oversight and reporting

## 4. Process Overview
1. **Invoice Receipt and Validation**
   - Verify purchase order (PO)
   - Match invoice with goods receipt
   - Confirm approval authority

2. **Invoice Entry**
   - Enter invoice data in ERP system (SAP / Oracle)
   - Assign correct GL accounts and cost centers

3. **Payment Processing**
   - Generate payment proposals
   - Approve payment runs
   - Record payment confirmations

4. **Reconciliation & Reporting**
   - Monthly vendor reconciliation
   - Ageing report review

## 5. Controls
- 3-way matching (PO–GR–Invoice)
- Dual authorization for payments
- Segregation of duties
