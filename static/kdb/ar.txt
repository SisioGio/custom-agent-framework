# Accounting Manual – Accounts Receivable (AR)

## 1. Objective
Establish uniform practices for invoicing customers, collecting payments, and recording receivables to ensure proper cash flow management.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all sales transactions and customer billing across all business units.

## 3. Key Responsibilities
- **AR Specialist:** Invoice creation, cash application
- **AR Manager:** Credit control and dispute management
- **Controller:** Review and aging analysis

## 4. Process Overview
1. **Customer Setup**
   - Verify creditworthiness
   - Create customer master data in ERP

2. **Invoicing**
   - Generate invoices from sales orders
   - Send via EDI/email to customers
   - Track due dates

3. **Collections**
   - Monitor overdue balances
   - Issue reminders and dunning letters
   - Coordinate with Sales for dispute resolution

4. **Cash Application**
   - Record incoming payments
   - Match to invoices
   - Handle unidentified payments

5. **Reporting**
   - AR aging report
   - Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) tracking

## 5. Controls
- Credit limit checks
- Review of overdue accounts
- Segregation of duties between invoicing and collection
