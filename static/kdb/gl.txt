# Accounting Manual – General Ledger (GL)

## 1. Objective
Ensure accurate and timely recording of all financial transactions in accordance with IFRS/GAAP and company policies.

## 2. Scope
Covers all general ledger entries, journal approvals, accruals, and closing activities.

## 3. Key Responsibilities
- **GL Accountant:** Journal entry posting and reconciliations
- **GL Supervisor:** Period close coordination
- **Financial Controller:** Final review and financial reporting

## 4. Process Overview
1. **Journal Entries**
   - Prepare manual journals with supporting documentation
   - Ensure proper account coding and approvals

2. **Reconciliations**
   - Perform monthly reconciliations of all balance sheet accounts
   - Investigate variances

3. **Month-End Close**
   - Accrual postings
   - Depreciation runs
   - Intercompany recharges

4. **Financial Reporting**
   - Prepare trial balance
   - Review profit & loss and balance sheet statements
   - Submit reports to HQ

## 5. Controls
- Journal entry approval matrix
- Automated reconciliation review
- Monthly close checklist
